# Business brief — UPI Pulse: India's Digital Payments Intelligence Platform

## Stakeholders
- Business owner / operations lead
- Data or analytics team
- Risk, compliance or customer-experience stakeholders as applicable

## Decision supported
Helps a payments operations team prioritise failure hotspots, identify high-value merchant segments and reduce avoidable payment friction.

## Success measures
- Payment success rate
- Failure rate by bank / PSP
- Merchant TPV
- Repeat payer rate
- Failure value at risk

## Validation plan
1. Agree metric definitions and data contracts with stakeholders.
2. Establish a baseline over a representative period.
3. Validate data completeness and segment coverage.
4. Run a limited pilot and review errors with domain experts.
5. Quantify impact, operational cost and unintended consequences.
